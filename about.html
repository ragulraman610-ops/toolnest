ABOUT TOOLNEST

Useful things, kept simple.

ToolNest is a small collection of everyday tools for quick calculations, writing checks, and image tasks.

What you'll find here

The site currently includes age, percentage, GST, and EMI calculators; a word counter; a QR generator; an image resizer; and an image compressor. The calculators, word counter, and image tools run in the page in your browser. The QR generator uses an external service to make its image.

There are no accounts or message-submission forms in the current site. ToolNest is intended as a convenient aid; calculator outputs are estimates and should be checked against the details relevant to your situation.

How the tools work

Most tools use the values you enter in the current page and do not need a server to calculate a result. The image tools use your browser to read an image and render an output. For QR generation, the text or link you enter is sent to api.qrserver.com as part of the image request.

Some visual typography is loaded from Google Fonts. See the Privacy page for the site's current external requests.

Questions or feedback
